How this works

Takes grain out of stock, adds the milled product to stock, and carries the whole cost into the milled product: grain cost + electricity. Any weight lost makes the milled kg cost more. Record your full electricity bill (lights, incubators, everything) in Expenses as KPLC / Utilities. The milling share is shown separately as "absorbed into stock", so nothing is counted twice.
