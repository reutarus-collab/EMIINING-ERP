Bag Prices (admin only)

Each item has its own bag sizes: wheat bran can be a 45 kg bag and another item a 40 kg bag. Press + Add bag size, enter the kg in the bag and the price for ONE bag. Leave an item with no bag rows if it is only sold by the kg. The per-kg price is set under Retail Pricing. A bag price must be above the cost of the bag.
