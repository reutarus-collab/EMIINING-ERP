EMINING ERP — WUFFDA/SID import patch

Upload this ZIP to /home/REUTARUS/EMIINING-ERP and run:
  cd ~/EMIINING-ERP
  unzip -o ~/EMINING-ERP-wuffda-sid-import.zip -d ~/EMIINING-ERP

Then open the PythonAnywhere Web tab and Reload the web app.

The Formulation Lab ingredient importer reads the WUFFDA Ingredients sheet;
the requirement importer reads Nutrients, lets you choose Current or a saved
specification, and previews mapped limits before saving. Bare amino acid
headings map to SID; explicit Total headings remain total.
